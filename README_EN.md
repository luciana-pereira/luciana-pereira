## 👩‍💻 About Me
I am a **Software Engineer** with over **5 years of experience** in developing, evolving, and maintaining mission-critical enterprise applications, currently focusing my career transition on **Solution Architecture**.

I work as a **Mid-Level Full Stack Developer at Avanade**, focusing on the **financial sector**, contributing to backend development, API design, complex system integrations, and building scalable, resilient systems.

My technical background includes **C#/.NET, JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Angular, Node.js, Java, and Python**, alongside relational and NoSQL databases, cloud computing, containerization, and DevOps practices.

I have been directing my professional growth toward **Solution Architecture and Software Engineering**, expanding my expertise in distributed systems, enterprise architecture, artificial intelligence, data engineering, and project management.

I believe great solutions go beyond code implementation: they require architectural vision, engineering quality, collaboration, and alignment with business objectives.
